name: research-idea-design-falsification
description: Check whether a research idea has a meaningful distinguishing consequence. Use during review to assess causal reasoning and simple alternatives without demanding experiments at the idea stage.
---

## Input

Use the idea, motivation, source evidence and technical references. This skill supplies internal reasoning, not scientific evidence. Do not execute experiments or access external tools.

## Procedure

1. Identify the central explanation and the important capability or understanding it would add. A detailed protocol cannot compensate for a trivial research question.
2. Consider the strongest simple alternative. Determine whether it already explains the proposed benefit or achieves the intended capability with the same available information.
3. State the observable consequence that would distinguish these explanations. Keep this to one conceptual sentence; do not design a sample, task instance, metric threshold, budget or experiment matrix.
4. If the argument is weak, improve the insight or propose a stronger angle within the user's direction. Do not invent artificial difficulty to sustain the original idea.
5. Treat missing results as normal at the idea stage. Only consequential contradictions in the idea or required information warrant a revision.

## Output

Return only the review task JSON. Use motivation_assessment.decisive_test for a concise distinguishing consequence, not an execution plan. Any issue needs a concrete improvement to the research argument. Do not request a full experimental protocol or numerical power analysis.

## Evidence discipline

Source findings need the supplied references. Hypotheses and predictions remain proposed reasoning. A related title does not settle overlap; judge the specific problem, mechanism, information and tested claim. A positive review does not establish empirical success.
